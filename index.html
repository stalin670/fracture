▲ FRACTURE

A neon 2D physics sandbox. Build, break, burn, drown, detonate.

Tools

  • Grab — drag & fling bodies
  • Spawn — drop shapes; drag for slingshot
  • Delete — remove on click/drag
  • Explosion — radial blast
  • Fire — ignite flammables, spreads
  • Water — pour liquid (SPH)
  • Rope / Weld — link two bodies
  • Blaster — drag-aim, release to fire
  • Ragdoll / Vehicle — spawn constructs

Controls

  • 1–9 select tools
  • Scroll zoom · Right/Middle/Alt-drag pan
  • WASD pan camera
  • ← → drive last vehicle
  • Space pause · F freeze · R clear
  • [ ] time scale · M minimap

Materials

wood burns · tnt cooks off · glass shatters · ice slides · rubber bounces · metal is heavy. Everything is destructible under enough force.

Built as a self-contained engine — custom rigid-body solver, fracturing, SPH fluid, fire propagation.
